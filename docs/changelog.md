# Changelog

- **1.5.8**:
  - Now Playing: a long song title or artist waits 1.5 s at its start before it scrolls, as a long album name does, so you can read its beginning.
  - Now Playing: Enlarged Album Art only appears over the art page. It no longer covers the lyrics, info or visualizer pages ([#57](https://github.com/DiamondBond/q2-pod/issues/57)).
  - Home: in Split, the album art follows a song change again. It could stay on the previous song's cover until two more skips.
  - Home: in Split, the panning album art stays evenly sharp. In 1.5.7 it turned soft and sharp again about once a second, most visibly in lettering on the cover.
  - Display: **Appearance → Centred Now Playing**, off by default, uses Shanling's own Now Playing layout: the art large in the middle, with the title and artist centred under it. Lyrics, the visualizer, the heart and ratings work there too; the track count, album line and remaining time are only on the iPod layout. Its progress bar takes your accent and Theme, as the iPod layout's does. It applies the next time Now Playing opens.
  - Classic: the Home menu and the Now Playing title use a bold font, cut from Liberation Sans Bold. A title with characters that font lacks (Chinese, Japanese or Korean, say) stays in the regular font.
  - View Queue: with Shuffle turned on from Now Playing, View Queue lists the songs in the order they'll play, not the library's order. The order starts fresh, so a song already played can come up again.
  - Now Playing: the Play/Pause hold menu ends with **More…**, Now Playing's own menu.
  - Display: choosing a Theme loads its look. **Classic**: Light Mode on, Cobalt, square corners, Home Split. **Minimal**: Light Mode off, Graphite, rounded corners, Home Split. Light Mode's Auto, the battery style and Centred Now Playing stay as you set them, and you can change any of them afterwards.
  - Home: in Classic, the longer bold labels fit their rows, so "Now Playing" no longer ends in "…".
  - Now Playing: in Centred Now Playing the play mode button sits level with the heart, the same distance from its edge.
  - Display: **Controls → Smart Awake**, off by default. While a song plays, the screen stays on while you're on the lyrics, info or visualizer page. When paused, it turns off as usual.
  - Lyrics: one click of the wheel no longer stops the lyrics following the song. It nudges them a little, and they spring back when you let go. Turn the wheel two clicks or more to scroll them yourself.
  - Wheel QS: Quick Settings follows your swipe across the wheel as it happens, instead of sliding open after you lift your finger. A swipe that turns into a turn of the wheel puts it back.
  - Buttons: after you hold the top button (Back), a second press it sometimes sends as you let go is ignored.
  - Buttons: a Centre press made while Play/Pause, Previous or Next is held is ignored, as it already was for Back. Pressing the wheel's rim near a corner no longer opens the Shut Down dialog when it also catches the Centre button.
  - Library: an empty `database.ignore` file in a folder keeps that folder's songs out of Songs, Albums, Artists, Coverflow and library shuffle, and from the displayed Favourites, Recent and Frequent lists (the records themselves are kept), while playlists and Folder still play them; a `database.unignore` deeper down shows a subfolder again. Update Local Music after adding or removing one. See [Hide folders from library browsing](discovery.md). From [@czdaniel's PR #144](https://github.com/DiamondBond/q2-pod/pull/144).
  - Scrobbling: with `AUTO=1` under `[SCROBBLE]` in `.scrobble.ini`, the Q2 uploads your listens by itself while it is on Wi-Fi. A single listen goes up within about three hours; the more you listen, the sooner, down to hourly. **Upload Scrobbles** still sends them at once.

- **1.5.7**:
  - Buttons: a Centre press made while the top button (Back) is held is ignored, so a loose wheel that closes both on one press only goes back.
  - Home: in Split, the album art pans smoothly instead of moving in small steps.
  - EQ: Simple has a **Presets** button, between the switch and Reset, which is now a small **R** box. It opens Advanced's stock presets (Pop, Rock and the rest). A preset picked there plays at once in Advanced, and Simple turns off. While a preset or Advanced plays, Simple's switch shows the preset's name and the sliders are dimmed. Tapping the switch then turns the EQ off. Moving a slider turns Simple back on.
  - Coverflow: swipe the covers. They follow your finger, glide on after a flick and slow to a stop on a cover. Tap a side cover to bring it to the middle, tap the middle one to open it, and touch the covers while they move to stop them. An artist's albums stretch a little past either end. The album name now changes as each cover passes the middle.
  - Coverflow: an album's song list takes taps and swipes. A tap plays the song, and a swipe up or down scrolls a long list. Before, only the wheel worked there.
  - Settings: the slider and the minus and plus buttons on Resume Songs, Crossfade and Wheel Sensitivity work by touch again. They only worked with the wheel while Default Volume was off.

- **1.5.6**:
  - Now Playing: Enlarged Album Art (10s wait) is off by default. The new default applies after a reset or on a new setup; a saved setting is kept.
  - Home: in Split, the album art drifts slowly across its panel, as on an iPod classic. It moves only while Home is on screen.
  - Bluetooth: gapless changes no longer click. For songs not at 48 kHz, the Q2 dropped about 0.2 ms of audio at every change between songs; it now plays through.

- **1.5.5**:
  - Bluetooth: skipping a track no longer crackles. The Q2 sent the headset nothing for about 0.4 s during a skip, so its buffer ran dry and the next song played on with it near-empty. After 0.1 s it now keeps the stream going with silence until the next song starts.
  - Search: in Light Mode, the letter you are typing in the search box is readable. It was white on light blue.
  - Now Playing: the full-screen art takes the cover's own shape, so a wide or tall cover fills the screen instead of shrinking into a square.
  - Coverflow: a fast spin starts jumping by letter a little later, after about 0.7 s of turning.
  - Crossfade: up to 15 s, was 10.
  - App Store: the page is titled **App Store**, was **Q2 App Store**.

- **1.5.4**:
  - Player: starting playback from stopped, and resuming from pause, answer at once instead of waiting up to a second.
  - Player: a track change without Gapless, and a folder's move to its next track, start as soon as stock's did.
  - Player: a CUE track's change is no longer delayed by the player's status checks.
  - EQ: the filter memory carries over only between gapless tracks, so a normal track change starts clean.
  - Playlists: a name already used by another playlist is refused as a whole name.

- **1.5.3**:
  - EQ: gapless albums no longer click between tracks while EQ or PEQ is on. Each track's filter started with empty memory instead of carrying on from the last one (#137).
  - MP3: gapless albums no longer tick between tracks. The player dropped each MP3's last frame, cutting the end of the track short (#135).
  - Now Playing: the wheel highlight on More's playback-speed buttons fits the visible button instead of covering the note below it (#135).
  - Clock: prevents background time conversions from briefly corrupting the status bar time, Auto Light Mode and timezone offsets.
  - Battery: the player sleeps while paused or stopped instead of checking for a command 50 or 100 times a second; it wakes for the command itself. Play, Pause and skips answer as before.
  - Battery: the status bar's clock and Auto Light Mode read the time once a minute instead of on every frame.
  - Performance and battery: with Gapless on, the interface asks the player for its status 10 times a second instead of on every frame, up to 125 times a second, waiting for each answer. Scrolling and animations no longer stall on it.
  - Playlists: creating a playlist no longer reuses an occupied ID after sorting by name, and database failures show an error.
  - Playlists: Delete Playlist removes the playlist and its entries in one step, keeps More open on Cancel, refreshes the list after success and reports failures. Music files and the current playback queue stay intact.

- **1.5.2**:
  - MP3: removes encoder delay and trailing padding from files with supported Xing/Info and LAME-compatible tags, preserving intentional silence. Keep Audio settings → Gapless on for continuous playback between tracks. Files without usable tags keep stock playback.

  - FLAC: local FLAC files play through faster decode paths, on by default.
  - Battery: the audio driver sleeps while playback is stopped instead of checking every 10 ms. Playback is unchanged.
  - iPod: the status bar's play/pause icon changes as soon as you press Play/Pause on Now Playing (#116). It often kept the old icon until you left Now Playing.
  - Battery: the rotator's clock starts only when the rotator first needs it, not at boot, and the backlight's PWM clock is released while the backlight is off.

  - iPod: **Light Mode**, under Settings → Display → Appearance, below Theme. It is off by default. It turns the whole interface light, in Minimal and Classic and under every accent: light backgrounds with dark text and icons, including stock's pages, pop-ups, Quick Settings, Cover Flow, games, the PEQ and the visualizer.   - iPod: the Green accent is the green Q2's sage (#119), as close to the shell as white text on it allows.

- **1.4.9**:
  - Videos: Library's Videos row opens the Videos folder in Folder view, as Podcasts and Audiobooks do, so each subfolder is its own row and videos open from their folders, instead of every video in one flat list.
  - SyncLink: Eddict Player's play/pause, previous and next work while the Q2's screen is off with Buttons Lock on (#113). The Q2 treated them as button presses, so the lock blocked them; it now locks only the buttons.
  - Tidal: long playlists, albums and My Collection's Tracks really do load past 50 now; the first 1.4.8 build still stopped at 50.
  - iPod: an album with no artwork shows a sharp disc in the album grids (Albums, an artist's albums) instead of a jagged one. The placeholder was 110 pixels and the Q2 shrank it to the 98-pixel cover roughly; it's now made at 98.
  - iPod: Now Playing's page dots stay put while you swipe between the art, lyrics, info and visualizer pages. Now and then they moved sideways with the page.
  - iPod: Now Playing's lyrics page shows the lyrics in large type: the line being sung in the middle, the one before above it and the next one below. Every line is the same size, set in heavy type like Apple Music's; a long one wraps onto more rows, and a line cut off at the top or bottom of the screen fades out. The line being sung lights up white. When the lyrics time each word (the "enhanced LRC" format, a `<mm:ss.xx>` before each word, in a .lrc file or a LYRICS tag), it lights word by word as each word is sung, as in Apple Music; lyrics timed only by the line, as LRCLIB's are, light the whole line at once. Lines now change to the hundredth of a second given in the lyrics, where they used to change on the whole second, up to a second early. A click's worth of wheel turn moves a whole line.

  - iPod: under **Corners: Rounded**, a row lights up as the same inset pill as the selection while you touch it, instead of a full-width square.
  - Resume: after a Network Update, a TF card update or Restart, the Q2 comes back to the song and the second you were playing. Stock saved that place only when the Q2 powered off, so these went back to wherever it was at the last power-off, however long ago.
  - Time zones (#108): the list runs west to east, from GMT-12 to GMT+14, and every row reads the same way, like "GMT-6 Chicago", short enough to show in full. New rows: Chicago (US Central), Phoenix, Kolkata, Brisbane and Auckland. Mexico City and Tehran no longer jump an hour for daylight saving they dropped in 2022, and Fiji no longer does either. The GMT-12 row is really GMT-12 now; stock set Kwajalein, which is GMT+12. Stock's GMT+6 row is now Dhaka, since Almaty moved to GMT+5. A row shows its standard offset: a place on daylight saving, like Anchorage, New York or Chatham, runs an hour ahead of it for part of the year (Lord Howe half an hour). Any other zone you'd already picked stays picked.

- **1.4.8**:
  - Spotify: when Spotify refuses to unlock your account's tracks, the Q2 now says so ("Spotify refused this account") within a second, instead of "Loading…" and, 15 s later, "Playback didn't start". It also stops librespot skipping through your whole playlist meanwhile. Spotify does this to some accounts, mostly new ones, and nothing on the Q2 can change it; another Premium account may work.
  - Battery: Tidal's helper sleeps until a Tidal track is asked for and goes back to sleep once it starts. Before, opening Tidal once kept it waking 5 times a second, through local music too, until Stop Streaming Services. A linked Bluetooth device whose buttons the Q2 can't find no longer wakes the player 5 times a second, and the player stops checking the memory card every 5 seconds while the screen is off.
  - Battery: a Bluetooth headset that doesn't report its own volume is asked for it every 2 seconds instead of 2.5 times a second. With the screen off and a volume limit set, the Q2 checks the headset's volume once a second instead of 2.5 times, so the headset's own buttons can go past the limit for up to a second before it's pulled back. Spotify's browser left open with the screen off no longer keeps Spotify running: after 30 seconds paused (2½ minutes if nothing has played yet) it closes and Spotify stops, as when you leave the browser. A video paused for 30 seconds now closes, keeping your place, instead of after 5 minutes, so the screen can turn off. If the memory card keeps refusing to save the queue, the Q2 tries again every 30 seconds instead of every 5.
  - Tidal: playlists, albums and My Collection's Tracks show every track, up to 2,000, instead of stopping at 50. Stock asks Tidal for 50 at a time; playlists and albums never asked for more, and favourite tracks only did when you dragged the touchscreen to the end of the list. A long list takes longer to open, as each 50 is another request.
  - Tidal Downloads: a track played from the card shows its artist and album, and its cover for FLAC (Lossless and above), instead of Unknown Artist and the placeholder (#102). Tidal sends its files without tags, so each download is now tagged from the Tidal track before it's saved. AAC downloads (Low and High quality) get the names but no cover. Tracks downloaded before this stay untagged until Clear All Downloads.
  - Library: Playlists are listed alphabetically, not in the order they were made. Add to Playlist's list follows the same order.
  - Folders: Shuffle, Play Next and Add to Queue on a held folder take in its subfolders too, so a folder of artists or albums shuffles everything in it. Before, they only took songs sitting directly in that folder, so a folder holding only subfolders left the queue unchanged. A very large folder takes a moment to gather.
  - 2038: secure connections (scrobbling, Internet Radio, Update, Apps, lyrics and covers) keep working once the certificates built into the firmware expire, from 2035, and after the clock passes January 2038, where the Q2's 32-bit clock overflows. The iPod status bar clock and scrobble times stay right past 2038 too.
  - iPod: a new accent, **Green**, after the green Q2. It comes after Plum in the Accent row.
