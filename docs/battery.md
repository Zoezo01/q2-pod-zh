# Replacing the battery

The step-by-step guide, with photos: [battery-replacement.pdf](battery-replacement.pdf).

## Tips from Shanling

- **A decent suction cup is all you need.** The back panel is glued to the frame, so the first opening takes a little more force.
- **Control your pull.** Three cables join the back panel to the main board: lift slowly so they don't tear the moment the glue gives.
- **The plastic tabs hold up.** Nothing pulls on them in normal use, and they survive being opened a few times. Cracking the whole back panel by forcing it is the more common mistake.
